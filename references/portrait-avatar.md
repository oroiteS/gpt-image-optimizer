### 【猫耳针织自拍·八段卡片模板】
适用：可爱系自拍/头像，日式八段固定卡槽填空模板

```text
Subject:
Selfie in cat ear knit outfit.

Main Subject:
A woman taking a selfie while bending forward in a white cat room, wearing cat ears and a tail, positioned in the center of the frame.

Character & Expression:
Small oval face, large dark brown eyes, thin eyebrows, well-defined nose, glossy pale pink lips. Expression is a soft smile, facing forward, looking at the camera through the smartphone. Long dark brown wavy hair let down, with thin bangs and a white-brown cat ear headband on top of the head.

Clothing & Pose:
Beige and pale pink open-knit long-sleeve knit dress, deep neckline, front tie, pom-pom decorations, short length, white cat-shaped slippers, long white-brown cat tail from waist. Knees bent, bending deeply forward, holding a smartphone with pink case in front of face with right hand, left hand resting on left knee.

Background & Light:
Foreground has long-pile white rug and cat teaser toy. Background has white shelves, peach blossoms, framed cat pictures, cat tower on the right, white-grey cat on floor. Soft warm indoor light from above and back-right illuminates face, knitwear, and cats.

Composition & Camera:
3:4 vertical composition, full-body portrait captured by front camera slightly lower than waist, from cat ears to white slippers. Person placed large in center, occupying most of frame height. Smartphone and tail included in frame, focus on face, smartphone, and cat ears, background lightly blurred.

Texture & Style:
Photorealistic live-action photo. High-definition natural skin, hair, clothing materials, surrounding props, maintaining soft indoor colors of white, beige, and pale pink.

Negative:
Change of cat ears/tail and forward bend; omit background cats.
```

**点评**：Subject / Main Subject / Character & Expression / Clothing & Pose / Background & Light / Composition & Camera / Texture & Style / Negative 八段固定卡槽，一段管一个维度，段名即检查清单，是最可复用的自拍/头像填空模板。

### 【自然 SNS 人像·反 AI 感指南】
适用：要"真实不要精修"的日常人像，整套反 AI 感约束包直接附加

```text
# Realism Guidelines

Apply these as the default aesthetic target.

## Basic mindset

- Make the result feel like a natural photo of a clearly adult woman in her 20s that could plausibly appear on a real SNS feed.
- Suppress CG-like texture, aggressive beauty filtering, plastic skin, and unnatural shine.
- Preserve attractiveness without making the face or skin impossibly perfect.
- Avoid fashion-campaign or advertisement styling; favor ordinary daily-life photography.
- Prioritize real-world presence, lived-in detail, and natural imperfection over perfection.

## Photography and texture

- Prefer an everyday smartphone snapshot rather than a high-end camera look.
- Use natural window light or existing practical lighting in the scene.
- Allow mild hand-shake softness, smartphone sensor noise, modest sharpening artifacts, and slightly rough resolution.
- Keep framing a little imperfect: slight tilt, uneven negative space, minor cropping, or off-center composition.
- Avoid poses that look designed for a photoshoot; favor a caught-in-the-moment feeling.
- Do not overuse cinematic depth of field. Ordinary phones often keep more of the scene legible.

## Facial details

- Keep realistic facial proportions and bone structure.
- Do not enlarge the eyes. Preserve natural sclera ratio, eyelid thickness, and subtle left-right differences.
- Keep expressions restrained and spontaneous; a faint smile or relaxed neutral expression is preferable to a forced grin.
- Unless explicitly requested, avoid rigid straight-on passport framing.
- Preserve mild facial asymmetry around brows, eyes, cheeks, and mouth.

## Final touches

- Keep pores, fine facial hair, soft redness, tiny blemishes, and natural shadow variation where appropriate.
- Hands and fingers must have plausible count, joints, lengths, overlap, and grip.
- Keep the overall impression clean, approachable, and cute while remaining physically believable.
- Hair should contain flyaways, irregular clumps, slight frizz, and uneven strands rather than perfectly separated hair fibers.
- Use realistic body proportions consistent with the reference or request. Avoid exaggerated hourglass shaping or model-like body retouching.
- Favor the kind of cuteness that feels possible in real life rather than doll-like perfection.

## Common negative constraints

Avoid: CGI, 3D render, doll skin, porcelain skin, excessive skin smoothing, beauty-app face reshaping, huge eyes, perfectly symmetrical face, over-sharpened eyelashes, waxy highlights, glam studio lighting, commercial fashion campaign, artificial rim light, extreme bokeh, hyper-detailed pore texture, over-HDR, unreal anatomy, extra fingers, fused fingers, floating accessories, warped glasses, duplicated jewelry, fake text, malformed background objects.
```

**点评**：把"照片感"拆成可执行清单（毛孔/碎发/不对称/手部关节/轻度手抖），是处理"要真实不要精修"需求时可直接复用的完整约束包。
**来源**：GPT-Image2-Skill · No. 67 · @LunarXuan

## 避坑清单（组装该类 prompt 时逐条对照）

1. **身份保持放第一行**：图生图黄金法则——先声明必须保留什么（脸/发型/表情/识别点），再声明要改变什么。（freestylefly No. 96 点评）
2. **锁脸要双保险**：逐五官的正面清单（facial structure, eyes, nose, lips, jawline...）+ 逐项负面清单（no identity change, no beautify...），单靠一句"保持一致"必漂移。（ai-image-prompts 点评）
3. **防 AI 网红脸**：写 `natural asymmetry`（保留自然不对称）；负面清单按"身份漂移/皮肤塑料感/表情失控/构图裁切"分组排布，别一锅炖。（ai-image-prompts 点评）
4. **太完美反而像假人**：毛孔、雀斑、碎发（flyaways）、左右眼细微差异、手部关节都要点名保留——反 AI 感是"写进正向的保留项"，不是一句 "realistic"。（freestylefly 避坑指南摄影 + GPT-Image2-Skill No. 67 点评）
5. **变与不变划边界**：风格化（ caricature/手办/Q 版）允许夸张变形，但身份特征必须保留，并写明美学方向（"weird 而非 cute"）防模型滑向卖萌。（ai-image-prompts 点评）
6. **一致性必须显式写**：身份一致性技巧在全库只占 17%，人像类必写 `keep the same face / consistent identity` + 参考图角色声明，两者几乎总成对出现。（awesome-gpt-image-2-API-and-Prompts 全库实测统计）
7. **写实人像必给镜头语言**：85mm、f/1.8、shallow depth of field、razor-sharp focus on the eyes 在写实人像类出现率超 60%，是人像类默认配置。（awesome-gpt-image-2-API-and-Prompts 全库实测统计）
8. **Avoid 段封死卡通化**：kawaii、anime、beauty filter、fake AI-looking skin 写进独立 Avoid/Negative 段，防头像被二次元化或磨皮过度。（API Case 116 点评 + 负面约束写法）
